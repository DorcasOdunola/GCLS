Gamified • Cybernetic (Adaptive) • Engaging Learning

Learning, Made More Engaging.

GCLS is a gamified cybernetic learning system designed to help educators create classes and subjects, deliver interactive lessons, assess learning, track student progress, and provide an adaptive feedback.

The challenge & our approach

Making Learning More Engaging and Adaptive

Traditional digital learning platforms can provide access to lessons and assessments, but they may not provide enough motivation, meaningful progress feedback, or personalised support for different learners.

GCLS combines structured learning, assessment, gamification, progress tracking, and adaptive feedback in one system. Educators can create classes and subjects, provide lessons and quizzes, monitor student performance, and use learner results to guide the next learning step.
